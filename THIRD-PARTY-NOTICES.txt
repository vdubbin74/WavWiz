MP3 encoding (LAME)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOT bundled. WavWiz's FFmpeg build is LGPL decode-focused plus native FLAC and
AAC encoders for the CD ripper. MP3 ripping would need LAME (LGPL), which is
intentionally not shipped; choose FLAC or AAC instead.

AirPlay receiver (0.1.1): shairplay - wavwiz-airplay.exe
============================================================
The AirPlay speaker "WavWiz - Whole House" uses shairplay by Juho Vähä-Herttua and contributors (https://github.com/juhovh/shairplay,
commit 096b61ad14c90169f438e690d096e3fcf87e504e), unmodified, built by the WavWiz project for Windows x64 together with a small
WavWiz front end (wavwiz-airplay.c, MIT) into wavwiz-airplay.exe. It runs as a SEPARATE PROGRAM started by the WavWiz server; it is not
linked into WavWiz. Licenses (see LICENSE-shairplay.txt): shairplay library - GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or later
(COPYING.LGPLv2.1.txt); its ALAC decoder (David Hammerton) - MIT; crypto (axTLS, Cameron Rich) - BSD; http_parser (Joyent / Ryan Dahl) - MIT;
curve25519-donna (Google / Adam Langley) - BSD-style; ed25519 (Orson Peters) - zlib. The optional FairPlay/playfair part is NOT built.
Complete corresponding source: source\shairplay-096b61ad14c90169f438e690d096e3fcf87e504e.tar.gz, source\wavwiz-airplay.c and
source\build-receivers.sh (exact build commands) next to these licenses. You may rebuild and replace wavwiz-airplay.exe with your own build.
airport.key is the publicly known AirPort Express key shipped in the shairplay source; it is used only to answer AirPlay's device challenge.
AirPlay is a trademark of Apple Inc. WavWiz is not endorsed by or affiliated with Apple.
