WavWiz privacy note

No cloud account, no analytics, no telemetry. WavWiz doesn't open ports to the internet.
- WavWiz has no account, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, no advertising and no automatic update check.
- WavWiz does use the internet for the things you choose to use:
  - Internet radio: the station you play is streamed from that station's own server. The Radio finder looks stations up at Radio Browser.
  - Tag and cover lookups (Tag & cover fix): song details are looked up at MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive, and at AcoustID if you add your own API key.
  - Spotify Connect (off unless you switch it on): the Spotify app on your phone or PC streams through Spotify's servers as usual.
  When you don't use these, WavWiz talks only to the devices on your home network.
- WavWiz doesn't open ports to the internet. By default the server only listens on your private network address. For access away from home, use your own Tailscale.
- The web page, the visualizers, the fonts and all pictures are served by your own WavWiz server; nothing is loaded from a CDN.
- Your music files are only read, never changed unless you approve tag fixes that write to files. Small cover-art thumbnails are cached in the WavWiz data folder.
- Data kept on the server PC (C:\ProgramData\WavWiz): the library index, your settings, favorites, the administrator password (stored as a salted hash, never in clear text), pairing tokens (stored as hashes) and logs. Remove it all by uninstalling and deleting that folder.
- Data kept in your browser: color theme, panel style, visualizer choices, the checklist and what's-new "seen" flags. Nothing there is sent anywhere.
- Access needs the administrator password or a pairing code/token.
- Diagnostics reports are shown on screen and copied by you if you choose; WavWiz never uploads them.
